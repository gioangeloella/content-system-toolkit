# Definition of done: integrating AI writing tools into design workflows

A guide for design leadership to help drive content quality and accountability.

## Purpose

This guide outlines how to integrate an AI writing tool into your team's existing workflows. By consistently using the tool as a writing partner, designers can spend more time on complex problem-solving while ensuring all delivered copy is clear, consistent, and on brand.

## What we're asking of design leaders

Integrate the AI writing tool into your team's definition of done, and use your existing design critiques to help build content accountability. This doesn't require content expertise — just the willingness to ask one or two questions during design critique sessions.

## Defining done with AI

Add AI writing tool usage to your definition of done to help ensure all delivered copy is clear and on brand. A design flow is ready for review after the designer has verified the content using the AI writing tool.

## Integrating into design critique rituals

Use these nudges during design crits to build accountability:

| Scenario | Questions to ask designers |
|---|---|
| New component or modal | "What did the tool suggest when you ran this screen through it? Did it recommend a more concise version?" |
| Error states | "Did the tool flag any accessibility or character count issues with that error message?" |
| Pattern consistency | "How does this label align with the content standards the tool referenced?" |
| Polishing drafts | "This wording feels robotic or complicated. Can we run it through the AI tool to see if it can be improved?" |

Customize these questions to match your team's content standards and the specific AI tool you're using.

## Escalation model

Keep the admin work for leaders lightweight. Instead of managing a document or spreadsheet, use a partner-observation model:

**Flagging issues:** If you notice copy in a critique that feels robotic, inconsistent, complex, or doesn't meet your content standards, tag your content lead in the designated Slack channel with a link to the file and some context.

**Design leaders as content partners:** This turns design leaders into partners in observation. If a file is flagged, the content lead jumps in to help the designer optimize their prompts so the whole team can see the solution.

## The compound effect

By embedding the AI writing tool into your rituals, you will:

- **Scale quality:** Ensure that even without a dedicated content designer on every project, the work meets your bar for clear, human, and accessible content.
- **Hit AI efficiency goals:** Every time a designer uses the tool for an empty state, modal, or error message, you move closer to reducing repetitive content tasks.
- **Reduce downstream costs:** AI writing tools built to recommend concise copy can help reduce translation costs, since translations often increase word counts significantly.

## How to customize this template

1. Replace references to the AI writing tool with your specific tool name (e.g., your Gem, your Claude Project, your custom GPT, etc.).
2. Adjust the critique questions to reference your content standards and terminology.
3. Define your escalation channel (Slack channel name, content lead name).
4. Add your team's specific quality bar and content principles.
