# Content System Toolkit

I pitched and built a Content Design Architect role from scratch inside 
a product organization — defining the role, building the content system, 
and rolling it out across design and engineering.

Along the way, I built a set of strategic artifacts: One-pagers, rollout
plans, quality rubrics, governance models, and goal frameworks. This repo
is the generalized, reusable version of the work — without company
specifics and codified into templates anyone building a content system can
adapt.

## What's here

- **[Content System Strategy Template](content-system-strategy-template.md)**
  — A "one-pager" template for defining and launching a content system
  function. Aligns leadership, scopes the work, and communicates what a
  content system is and is not.

## Who this is for

Content designers moving into systems or architect roles. Design leaders
trying to scale content quality without scaling headcount. Anyone building
the infrastructure that lets product and engineering teams write well at 
scale.

## A note about the templates

These are frameworks, not prescriptions. Every organization's context,
mandate, and constraints are different. Take what's useful for your 
situation.
