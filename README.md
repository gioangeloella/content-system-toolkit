# Content System Toolkit

I'm building a Content Design Architect role from scratch inside 
a product organization — defining the role, building the content system, 
and rolling it out across design and engineering.

Along the way, I'm creating a set of strategic artifacts: One-pagers, 
rollout plans, quality rubrics, governance models, and goal frameworks. 
This repo is the generalized, reusable version of all the work — without 
company specifics and codified into templates anyone building a content 
system can adapt.

## What's here

- **[Content System Strategy Template](content-system-strategy-template.md)**
  — A "one-pager" template for defining and launching a content system
  function. Aligns leadership, scopes the work, and communicates what a
  content system is and is not.
- **[AI Writing Tool Scorecard](ai-writing-tool-scorecard.md)**
  — A measurement framework for tracking adoption and usage of AI writing tools across a product design team.
- **[AI Writing Tool Scorecard](content-system-roadmap.md)**
  — A two-horizon framework for building and scaling a content system.
- **[AI Writing Tool Scorecard](content-sytems-ownership-model.md)**
  — A framework for defining what a content systems function owns, partners on, and does not do.
- **[AI Writing Tool Scorecard](definition-of-done-guide.md)**
  — A guide for design leadership to help drive content quality and accountability.
- **[AI Writing Tool Scorecard](designer-feedback-survey.md)**
  — This is a question repo for gathering structured feedback from designers using an AI writing tool.

## Who this is for

Content designers moving into systems or architect roles. Design leaders
trying to scale content quality without scaling headcount. Anyone building
the infrastructure that lets product and engineering teams write well at 
scale.

## A note about the templates

These are frameworks, not prescriptions. Every organization's context,
mandate, and constraints are different. Take what's useful for your 
situation.
