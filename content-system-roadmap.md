# Content system roadmap

A two-horizon framework for building and scaling a content system.

## Purpose

A content system roadmap structured around horizons rather than quarters gives you the flexibility to sequence work by dependency rather than by calendar. Horizon 1 builds the foundation and keeps interim tooling alive. Horizon 2 scales the system and makes quality structural.

## The two-horizon model

### Horizon 1: Foundation + Bridge

Build the backbone while the bridge holds the team.

This horizon covers the work that needs to happen before anything can scale. You're updating standards, establishing governance, standing up interim tooling, and building trust with the team. The "bridge" is whatever's keeping content quality afloat while the system is under construction (e.g., an AI writing tool, office hours, manual reviews).

### Horizon 2: Scale + Sustain

Quality becomes structural, enforced by the system rather than by headcount.

This horizon extends content standards into engineering, evolves tooling from interim to integrated, and shifts quality enforcement from manual to systematic. The system starts carrying the load that people were carrying in Horizon 1.

## Workstreams

Organize milestones across four workstreams. Each maps to a distinct area of investment:

| Workstream | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Content system | Building and launching the system itself: standards, governance, quality rubrics, and the rollout to teams |
| Operational standards | Making standards machine-readable, AI-ready, and enforceable — evolving from documentation to infrastructure |
| AI content infrastructure | The AI writing tool, measurement, feedback loops, and the evolution toward integrated tooling |
| Operating model | The personal and role-level investment in technical fluency, engineering literacy, and cross-functional credibility |

## How to use the companion spreadsheet

The companion CSV ([content-system-roadmap.csv](content-system-roadmap.csv)) provides a fillable tracker with the following columns:

- **Horizon:** 1 or 2
- **Workstream:** Which of the four areas this milestone belongs to
- **Milestone:** What you're delivering
- **Target quarter:** When you're aiming to deliver it
- **Status:** Not started, in progress, complete
- **Dependencies:** What has to happen first
- **Notes:** Context, risks, or links to related artifacts

Start by filling in Horizon 1. Be specific about milestones and dates. Vague goals produce vague roadmaps. If you can't attach a deliverable and a quarter to a milestone, it's not ready to be on the roadmap yet.

## Tips

- **Goals should feed the roadmap.** If your goals have specific deliverables and dates baked in, the roadmap practically writes itself. Work backward from goals to milestones.
- **All artifacts should connect.** The listening tour informs the pitch. The pitch becomes the strategy doc. The strategy doc grounds the job description. The job description informs goals. Goals generate the roadmap. Each artifact feeds the next.
- **Horizon 2 is allowed to be rougher.** You'll know more by the time you get there. Define the direction and the key milestones, not every deliverable.
